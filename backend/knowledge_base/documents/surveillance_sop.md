# Surveillance Monitoring SOP

## Purpose

This fictional demonstration SOP defines a general
monitoring workflow for an AI-assisted surveillance
system.

## Detection

When an object is detected by the surveillance system,
the system should record the object category, confidence
score, timestamp, and tracking identifier when available.

## Verification

Automated detections should be treated as decision-support
information. An operator should verify significant events
before taking further action.

## Risk Assessment

Risk levels may be categorized as LOW, MEDIUM, or HIGH
based on the configured demonstration scoring system.

A risk score should not by itself be interpreted as proof
of hostile or malicious intent.

## Human Review

Events with elevated risk or unusual behavior should be
presented to an authorized human operator for review.

## Documentation

Significant events should be recorded with the available
detection, tracking, anomaly, and risk information.
