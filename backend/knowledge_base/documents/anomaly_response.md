# Anomaly Analysis Guidance

## Purpose

This fictional guidance describes the interpretation of
movement anomalies in an AI-assisted surveillance system.

## Low Anomaly

A LOW anomaly level indicates that the observed movement
does not significantly deviate from the configured
demonstration baseline.

## Medium Anomaly

A MEDIUM anomaly level indicates a noticeable deviation
from the configured movement pattern and should receive
additional operator attention.

## High Anomaly

A HIGH anomaly level indicates a substantial deviation
from the configured demonstration baseline.

A high anomaly score does not establish intent or threat
identity.

## Interpretation

Anomaly scores should be considered together with object
category, detection confidence, location, temporal context,
and other available information.

## Human-in-the-Loop

The anomaly engine is intended to assist human operators.
Final interpretation remains with the authorized operator.
